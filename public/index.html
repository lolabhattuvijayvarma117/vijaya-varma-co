About Us

Trust, Accuracy, &
Professionalism

Established in April 2013, we are one of the leading marine cargo surveyors in India, handling export-import cargo across various major ports.

Our organization is licensed by the Government of India under the Finance Act 1994. Headquartered in Kakinada, we maintain a vast network of branches delivering accurate and timely reports with the highest ethical standards.

  • Highly experienced executive team
  • Specialized field staff for all cargo types
  • Dedicated laboratory professionals
  • Timely and accurate reporting systems
