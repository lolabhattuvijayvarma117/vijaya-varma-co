About Us

Trust, Accuracy, &
Professionalism

Established in April 2013, we are one of the leading marine cargo surveyors in India, handling export-import cargo across various major ports.

Our organization is licensed by the Government of India under the Finance Act 1994. Headquartered in Kakinada, we maintain a vast network of branches delivering accurate and timely reports with the highest ethical standards.

  • Highly experienced executive team
  • Specialized field staff for all cargo types
  • Dedicated laboratory professionals
  • Timely and accurate reporting systems

Specialized Operations

Bulk Cargo Operations

Comprehensive supervision and analysis for all types of bulk cargo, ensuring accurate measurements and quality control.

  • Draft Survey (Initial & Final)
  • Complete Discharge Supervision
  • Scientific Sampling
  • Chemical Analysis
  • Daily Status Reporting

Loading Operations

Rigorous monitoring and assessment during loading phases to maximize efficiency and maintain quality standards.

  • Thorough Wagon Inspection
  • Weight & Volume Assessment
  • Systematic Sampling
  • Moisture Analysis Tests

Bagged Cargo Operations

Detailed tracking and supervision for all bagged cargo handling, with strict emphasis on precise tallying.

  • Warehousing Supervision
  • Loading/Unloading Supervision
  • Accurate Tally of Bags
  • Comprehensive Reporting

Container & General Cargo

End-to-end supervision for containerized and general cargo across multiple transit modes.

  • Rail Movement Inspection
  • Road Movement Inspection
  • Stuffing/Destuffing Supervision
  • Quality & Quantity Verification
  • Safety & Handling Checks
